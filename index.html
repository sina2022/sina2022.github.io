About

I'm a software engineer with 7+ years of experience delivering enterprise applications end-to-end. My core is backend engineering in Java and Spring Boot, but I work across the whole stack — Angular and TypeScript on the front, MySQL and REST APIs in the middle, Docker and AWS around it.

Most of my career has been in enterprise healthcare, where correctness, data privacy, and uptime aren't optional. I enjoy the parts other people avoid: debugging production issues under pressure, modernizing legacy systems safely, and turning vague requirements into software that's actually maintainable.

Outside of client work, I build my own tools and open-source learning programs — including two full job-ready engineering courses and a market-analysis assistant. I like understanding things deeply enough to teach them.
